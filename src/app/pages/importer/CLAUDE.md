# src/app/pages/importer — the import shop's panel

The deepest lane in the mock-up, and the one the brief says to build first: `V·01` through `V·06`,
plus the catalogue it publishes and the drivers it hires.

| Screen            | Route                            | What it has to resolve                                            |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Panel de envíos   | `/importadora/envios`            | what is broken, on top, before what is normal                     |
| Alta y recojo     | `/importadora/envios/nuevo`      | one form, one price                                               |
| Cobro único       | `/importadora/envios/nuevo/pago` | three ways to pay, one of which moves the charge to the recipient |
| Detalle del envío | `/importadora/envios/:guia`      | everything needed when the buyer writes asking                    |

| Cuenta y cobros   | `/importadora/cuenta`            | what was spent and what is still to collect                       |

## The rules of this lane

- **The waybill is generated by the payment, not by the form.** `V·02` fills a draft held in
  `domain/draft.ts`; `V·03` is where the code comes into existence, and its alert says so.
- **What is broken goes above the filter.** `V·01` pins an `arena-alert` over the segmented control
  when a shipment has no driver, and its action opens the first one. That ordering is the brief's,
  and it is the whole reason the screen replaces a spreadsheet.
- **The dark band on `V·04` is ours, not a restyled app bar.** Darkening the bar through a part
  hook cannot reach the ink of everything inside it. It is `shared/shipment-header`, and **a
  status tag must not go inside it** — a neutral `ArenaTag` reads `--ink-body` and vanishes on ink.
- **"Reportar un problema" is outline.** Danger is never filled in Arena, and this is the only
  destructive action in the lane.
- **A batch shows the broken row before it shows a total.** `V·05` validates per line, marks the
  rows it cannot create, and only then offers to create the rest.
- **Every waybill, amount and hour is in the mono face.** A code column uses `mono: true`; a money
  or time column uses `align: 'right'` and `.arena-num` on the cell, because `mono: true` also
  carries the identifier ink and a total is not an identifier.
